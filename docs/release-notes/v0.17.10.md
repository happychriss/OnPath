# OnPath 0.17.10 – My routes with map pictures and favourites

**My routes**
- **Map pictures:** every route shows a small map with the whole route, its activity („Hike · T2", „Bike") and „↻ Round trip" when it returns to the start.
- **Favourites:** tap the star to keep a route; the „☆ Favourites" chip shows only those.
- **Filter by activity:** the chip starts with the activity you plan with; its menu holds the six activities and „All".
- On every opening: newest first, the chips at the start, and „Recent" (the plan you work on) always shown.

**Planning**
- **Search:** a found place opens the same menu as holding the map – including „Start here" on an empty plan.
- **Very long route → Cancel** brings the map back to your plan.
- **„Set as new destination“ while planning** keeps the old destination as the last stop – plan forward step by step. During a tour the new destination still replaces the old one.

**Map**
- **Big cities stay smooth:** restaurants, cafés and shops appear a little later when zooming in where there are very many.
- **Hiking route bands without dark spots** where pieces meet; streets on a route stay tinted green.
- The position dot is drawn by the map itself – less work for the phone.

**Recording**
- **A very short recording** (you never got more than 50 m from the start) goes to the recycle bin, with „Undo" right away and 30 days to restore.

**Colours**
- „Let's go" in a route's ⋮ menu is plan blue; the sheet under a recorded tour is light green like the plan sheet.
