# OnPath 0.10.3 – calmer, clearer, bigger where it matters

**One look for every message**
- Information is quiet grey text. A question is the blue bar with buttons. A warning is amber with ⚠. Something
  that failed is red and says what you can do.
- Buttons say what they do ("Route back", "Just record") instead of Yes / No; shorter texts everywhere.

**Navigate, readable at a glance**
- Bigger text: time to go, turn distance and arrival.
- Off route: one amber card – "Off route · 120 m north-east" with a big arrow – instead of three red notes.
- No GPS: one amber line "No GPS · position 4 min old".
- The recording line shows only when it matters (paused, no GPS); moving time and breaks are in the opened card.
- Your pace in words ("Faster than planned uphill").

**Plan and map**
- Map notes without a decision (offline, waiting for GPS) are a small grey label; the blue bar only asks.
- "Detailed map for here? · 9 MB" is short and no longer overlaps the "Overview only" note.
- Route problems in the plan sheet: one red line; "map loading" and "new route" are quiet notes.
- Progress (import, export) shows where you started it.

**Settings**
- Two groups: your settings, and App (Updates, Privacy, Diagnostics, About).
- Diagnostics is grey "For bug reports" – red only for a new problem.
- No more "Allow all the time" question; the hint stays in Settings → Navigation.
- Developer tools appear after tapping the version in About 7 times.
