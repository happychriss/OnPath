# OnPath 0.17.3 – one map orientation, Planen / Los geht's from Meine Touren, off-route in two steps, bike voice on time

**Map**
- **One map orientation for the whole session:** north up or walking direction – Plan, Navigate, fits and locate all
  keep the one you chose. The compass button is the only switch; no compass flash at the tour start.
- **Locate when you are off the map:** in Plan the locate button shows how far away you are (850 m, 3,2 km, 790 km);
  a tap shows the plan and you together.
- **Fits keep points clear of the buttons:** "Ganze Route" and the other fits no longer put a point under the activity
  button or the search pill.
- **The grey band names its T level** in red, and the T badge on the activity button is bigger.

**Plan**
- **"② erst ab T2 erreichbar":** a stop the route does not reach says what reaches it ("nur mit Gravelbike", "zu Fuß
  T2", "nicht erreichbar") instead of "kein Weg für Wandern". A tap on the line shows that section on the map.
- **Hold a place → "Als Zwischenstopp" / "Als neues Ziel"**, chosen by how clear the case is; the other one waits under
  "Mehr". During a tour a held place is always a stop on the way, never a new start.
- **The empty start sheet** shows three calm steps: Ziel finden › · Route anpassen · Los geht's.
- **Los geht's is one big green button** in the sheet's foot. Los geht's with a changed saved plan updates that route –
  no "Name (2)".
- Plan blue a tick lighter (it looked almost black on the phone). "Ohne Plan aufzeichnen" is now **"Nur aufzeichnen"**.

**Meine Touren**
- **Planen and Los geht's** on every planned route: in the row's ⋮, in the large overview (Abbrechen · Los geht's ·
  Planen) and Planen on Zuletzt rows.
- **A done tour keeps the plan it followed:** Gemacht → ⋮ / overview offers Planen and Los geht's for it; its line shows
  thin under the track.
- Every action in one place: Zuletzt keeps Speichern · Löschen on the row, Gespeichert and Gemacht are a clean list
  with ›. The ⤢ on the small picture opens the large overview and never covers the route.
- A route added during a tour now always shows in the list (a late, older read could hide it).

**Navigating**
- **Leaving the route in two steps, shown and said:** first the amber card "Abseits der Route", then the question. Each
  question is said, also the second and third in a row. On the bike, "back on the route" needs you clearly nearer – a
  fork beside the route no longer closes the question after three seconds.
- **New route that fits the way you walk:** if you walked on after the question, the new route is computed again from
  where you are now; a new route that would send you back over the metres just walked goes on ahead instead.
- **The activity switch far from the route:** only the activity you tap is routed, from the plan's own start – no more
  90 s waits routing from 700 km away.
- **A round trip ridden twice:** the second lap is followed from the start and its turns are said again.
- **Arrival box: "Weiter aufzeichnen" | "Tour beenden".** After the end you are back at the empty start, as after Neu
  planen.
- **Pause means quiet:** while paused no questions and no GPS warnings; a paper band says "⏸ Pause · 12:04". The
  questions come back after Weiter.
- **One hold unlocks the bar for 20 s;** "Weiter" always wants a hold, and into the pocket locks it at once. The hold
  fills by the clock – with animations off a short tap no longer fires it.

**Voice on the bike**
- **Each turn gets its own "Gleich …"**, heard about 6 s before the turn (the real delays counted); none is swallowed
  behind another sentence or after a stop.
- **Two turns close together along a street** ("right onto the road, 19 m on left") are said as two turns.
- **The goal's side:** "Ziel erreicht. Das Ziel ist rechts von dir." – within 15 m of the goal, not 30 m before it.

**Robust**
- **One app window:** a second OnPath screen at start stands down at once, and the sync files can no longer be lost
  to two writers ("Sync files could not be written").
- **Offline corridor by activity:** the map around the route is kept 10 km wide on foot, 20 km by bike.
- Settings saved off the main thread, a lighter recording tick, every tour starts fresh, and a watchdog that names
  what blocked the screen.
