# OnPath 0.17.2 – calmer look, plan and tour apart, hold to unlock, route previews

**Look**
- **Walnut, paper and ink blue:** the app's ground is paper, neutral buttons are walnut, everything that plans is ink blue,
  everything that takes you on the way is green. Red only for delete. No blue tinted surfaces and no blue glow in Plan any
  more – Plan is recognised by the PLANEN pill, the sheet's blue top edge and the blue buttons.
- **Warm shadows, one corner family, a blue compass needle** (north ink blue instead of red).
- **Numbers like an instrument:** big number, small unit, digits of equal width – nothing jiggles in Navigate.
- **The route draws itself** from start to goal when it is calculated, with a short tick at the end.
- **The goal shows a flag** instead of its number; the elevation profile is filled with a soft gradient, steeper parts
  stronger.
- **OnPath on the start screen** (paper, icon, wordmark), in the head of the ☰ menu and on Über OnPath.

**Plan and tour are two things**
- The plan is saved at **Los geht's**; ending a tour asks only about the tour. The plan stays on the map after the tour.
- **Zuletzt** in Meine Touren keeps your last 25 plans (no "Nicht gespeichert" questions any more). The plan you are
  working on is the first row, marked **In Bearbeitung** – tap it to go back to it.
- **Löschen** always moves to the recycle bin, without a question.
- During a tour, tapping a plan or a done tour in Meine Touren follows it at once – "Neuer Plan – deine Aufnahme läuft
  weiter" with **Rückgängig**.

**Meine Touren**
- **A small picture of every route** (planned, done, imported GPX). A tap opens an overview on the map, fitted to the
  whole route, with **Planen** / **Abbrechen** (during a tour: **Folgen**).

**Navigating**
- **Hold to unlock:** while locked, **Pause** and **Planen** stay where they are – hold a button for about a second, the
  colour fills it, then it acts. A short tap shows "Gedrückt halten". The hint line shows only until you have held once.
  How long to hold (Aus · 0,5 – 2 s) is in Einstellungen → Navigation. The slider is gone.
- **One bar with and without the lock:** Pause | Planen; paused: Weiter · Beenden · ✎. "Weiter geht's" takes you back
  from Plan to Navigate.
- **"Du stehst mitten auf der Route"** is asked once per plan; the way to ① holds until you reach ①.
- **Undo during a tour** back to one point where you stand ends the guidance quietly instead of following an old line;
  one point left during a tour no longer loops ("kein Punkt 1 mehr").
- **The top card** opens by a tap anywhere on it or by pulling it down.
- **⚙ on the way** opens the settings in the new look, the Navigation page first.

**Tour beenden**
- The end sheet ignores a press in its first half second (the thumb that just held Beenden).
- A tour without a recorded line shows "Keine Strecke aufgezeichnet" instead of "Als Plan übernehmen".
- "In Meine Touren ›" opens Meine Touren → Gemacht with the new tour marked.

**Plan**
- **Quiet about GPS:** no GPS warnings, no coloured dot on the locate button, no accuracy circle in Plan.
- **Nur Übersichtskarte · Detailkarte laden** – a paper pill that covers nothing.
- The plan sheet: a tap on its handle steps it bigger.

**Settings**
- Grouped pages with one row per setting and its current value; the choice opens below. **Handy einrichten** moves to
  the top with what is missing.

**Under the hood**
- No error is swallowed any more: background work reports to the problem log instead of stopping the app.
- One geo calculation instead of nine copies (navigation and routing now measure exactly alike).
- The plan, the tour and Meine Touren are their own tested parts (random-flow tests with fixed rules).
- Fewer, better tests: tests that only read the source text and English screenshot twins are gone.
