# OnPath 0.9.1 – fixes from the first hike with 0.9.0, a tidier plan sheet

**Fixed (field test 2026-10-22)**
- **Inns and other places no longer vanish at some zoom levels.** A trail number ("40") next to them used to win the
  space on the map; places now come first.
- **The recorded track no longer breaks into pieces** when GPS is poor for a while (forest, steep slopes). A gap of up
  to 5 minutes is bridged: the line stays connected and the distance counts. Every gap is noted in Settings → Diagnostics.
- **The "I am here" dot stays on top** of route points and lines, also after the map reloads (it slipped underneath).
  New tests check this on every build and on the emulator.
- **Navigate shows the numbered points** of the route you follow (they are not tappable there).

**Ending a tour**
- The question now always offers **Cancel · Delete · Save** (on arrival: Keep going · Delete · Save).
- **Delete** (red) asks once more, then the tour goes to the recycle bin (30 days) – handy for a short walk you don't
  want to keep.
- After Save or Delete the route leaves the planning screen – you are done with it. Undo brings it back.

**Plan sheet**
- Pulled up, the sheet is one scrolling page: **elevation profile first** – slide a finger along it and a dot runs along
  the route on the map – then the **points**, then **way types & surfaces**. Title line and the start button stay put,
  so 3 or 30 points work the same.
- **Reverse** and **Round trip** are now only above the points (no longer twice, in ⋯ too).
