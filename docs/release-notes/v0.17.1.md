# OnPath 0.17.1 – switching the activity works again, quieter guidance, Tour beenden on one screen

**Navigating**
- **Switching the activity during a tour works again:** Bike → Hike (or any other) → "Neue Route" – the tour is now
  guided for the new activity. Before, the old guidance came back right away.
- **The position stays where you are:** the dot is drawn on the route only within 6 m of it (was 25 m). On a road beside
  the planned cycle path the dot stays on the road.
- **"Weiter geht's"** – after you changed the plan during the tour, the voice says once how far it is now:
  "Weiter geht's. 9,5 Kilometer bis …, etwa 40 Minuten."
- **One turn after the other:** a turn is announced only after the one before it is ridden. "Gleich rechts halten,
  danach links" is no longer followed by "In 100 Metern rechts" while the left is still ahead.
- **Sperren | Plan:** after unlocking, "🔒 Sperren" and "Plan" are two equal buttons side by side.

**Ending a tour**
- **Tour beenden on one screen:** the numbers in one line, the big green **Tour speichern**, then **Weiterfahren** and
  **Löschen** side by side – nothing hidden below. Löschen moves the tour to the recycle bin (30 days).
- **A plan made on the phone is kept:** "Plan auch speichern" is now on for a plan you never saved, so it is in
  Meine Touren after the tour.

**Meine Touren**
- **Search:** a search box under Geplant | Gemacht finds a tour by any word of its name ("kofel", "koln" finds "Köln").
