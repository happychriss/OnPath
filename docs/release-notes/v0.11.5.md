# OnPath 0.11.5 – auf Deutsch / in German, greener region view

**Deutsch / German**
- OnPath speaks German: the app follows your phone's language (German or English). Activity names in German: Fahrrad,
  Gravelbike, E-Bike, MTB, E-MTB, Wandern.
- "Gravel bike" is now called **Gravelbike** in English too; "Slide to plan" is now **Edit route**.

**Map**
- The region view is greener: open land, meadows and forests read as green, towns stay grey.
- **Pink means long distance:** zoomed out, only long-distance bike and hiking routes are pink (wider, with a light
  edge); regional routes are a calm green line. Close in, only long-distance routes keep the pink-green band – for
  hiking too.
- Built-up areas are a little darker close in, so white streets stand out.
