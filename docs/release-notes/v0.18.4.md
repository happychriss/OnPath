# OnPath 0.18.4 – Holds and places that always land

**Planning**
- **A hold on the map always gives you a point** – also right after setting the first point, while its route is still being calculated (before, such a hold could add nothing, without a word).
- **A place taken from its menu shows its symbol at once** („Hier starten“, „Als Ziel“, „Hierhin“ on a restaurant, a hut …) – not only a number first.

**My routes**
- **„Zuletzt“:** a plan opened from „Zuletzt“ and changed updates its entry – no second draft, nothing goes to the bin. Undo back to an older version is kept.

**Planner (browser)**
- A click while the route is still being calculated is decided again on the plan as it is now – it always lands.
