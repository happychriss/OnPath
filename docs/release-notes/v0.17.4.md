# OnPath 0.17.4 – a voice on every phone, the far destination kept, relief that knows a slow phone, ready for Google Play

**Voice**
- **The voice step on any phone (Samsung too):** the test never stays silent – it says what happened (spoken, muted,
  no voice, error). When the phone's own speech engine has no German or English voice, OnPath uses Google's speech
  services directly. The step names the engine and has buttons to the right place: Load voice, Speech Services by
  Google, Speech output settings; it checks again when you come back.
- **A tour without a working voice vibrates the turns** and the "Abseits der Route" step, instead of staying quiet.

**Plan**
- **A far destination is no longer lost:** "Cancel" at the very-long-plan question, or while the map for it loads,
  keeps the found place's pin on the map; a fly to the place no longer jumps back to your first GPS fix.
- **An unsaved plan is never lost at "Neu planen" or the tour end:** it is in Zuletzt at once, the place name follows.
- **Saved routes open without routing again:** a route from Meine Touren is on the map at once, as it was saved.
- Meine Touren offers Speichern only when the plan itself changed (also after pairing with the browser).
- The hint "Halte die Karte gedrückt …" sits above the low sheet's foot, no longer hidden behind it; route warnings
  start with a capital letter ("Ford · 5 steep sections").
- Changing the activity or the T level yourself in Plan no longer shows "Neue Route … bitte prüfen" – that line
  stays for changes you did not make (a re-route on the way, the browser).
- The GPS note in Plan sits beside the activity button and no longer covers the T badge; at large font it wraps
  instead of cutting letters off.

**Map**
- **Relief switches itself off once on a slow phone** (the map stutters while you move it), with a short hint on the
  map. Your own choice in the layers always wins.
- **Map credit "© OpenStreetMap · OpenMapTiles"** on the map; a tap opens Settings → About.
- **Map store:** no red error notice while old map versions are cleaned up, fewer rewrites of the large map files,
  and the map never waits for a file read on the main thread.

**Navigating**
- **Navigate keeps the followed route line** when you open a done tour in Meine Touren during a tour – no more points
  without a route.
- **A new route after leaving the plan never drops a stop unasked:** on an out-and-back stretch the stop ahead is
  no longer counted as passed – the new route goes via it, or asks "Über ②" / "② auslassen".
- **Arrival after a plan edit near the goal:** "Ziel erreicht" and the end box come also when the plan changed in
  the last metres; on the bike at most 5 s after the last GPS fix, even when no new fix comes.
- **The arrival time near the goal is the real estimate** – no more "4 min, Ankunft 11:15" 49 m before the goal.
- Riding on after the arrival no longer pauses the trip time estimate.
- The "Tour unterbrochen" notification goes away once you answer it (Weiter, Speichern, Löschen).
- The ride summary follows a language switch made while it is open.
- "Tour beenden" and a new Los geht's right after it: the new tour keeps recording.
- A demo ride ends with its tour – "DEMO" and the Sim chip no longer stay.

**Google Play**
- **OnPath is in Google Play's internal test:** the Play build has no own updater (Play updates it) and targets
  Android 16 (targetSdk 36).
- **Planner links open in the app** on Play installs too (plan.onpath.earth), not in the browser.
- The developer tools in Settings are hidden (seven taps on the version show them).
