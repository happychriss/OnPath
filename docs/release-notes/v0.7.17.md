# OnPath 0.7.17 – green ways for walking and cycling

- **Thin green lines show ways made for you**, like Komoot – depending on the activity:
  - **Hiking:** footways and pedestrian zones (and shared foot-and-cycle paths).
  - **Bike / e-bike / MTB:** cycleways, and footways or pedestrian zones where bikes are allowed.
  Good for planning in towns: green = you can walk / ride well there. Works with the map you already have (no new
  map download).
- **Fix: pedestrian zones were missing on the map since 0.7.0** (e.g. the Passerpromenade) – drawn again.
