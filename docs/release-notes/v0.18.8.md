# OnPath 0.18.8 – Arrival, and a closer look at symbols

**Navigation**
- **Arrival no longer waits for a stop the route cannot reach** – once you have been at the place on the route closest to that stop, it no longer holds back „Du bist angekommen“.

**On the map**
- **Symbols that answer late can now be traced** – the app notes what happens when you tap or hold a symbol, without places or names. Tapping and holding work as before. If it happens again, export under Settings → Diagnostics.
