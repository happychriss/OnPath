# OnPath 0.16.0 – a round trip stays a round trip, offline at a glance, new words Plan · Tour · Route

**Round trips and the start**
- **Every tour starts at point ①.** "Hinführen" now always leads to point ① first and then follows the whole plan –
  a round trip is no longer entered between ① and ② (the bug of 3 November).
- **Standing in the middle of the route at the start** asks: **"Du stehst mitten auf der Route."** –
  **Zu ① führen** or **Hier einsteigen** (also on the lock screen).
- Points count **only in their order**: a later point you pass by chance does not skip the ones before it; a round
  trip's end point is not "reached" at the start.
- **A point set on the way to a round trip** (the bakery) becomes a **stop on the way** – the loop 1 → 2 → 3 → 1
  stays. "Hier starten & enden" turns only the loop.
- A plan loaded during a tour starts at its point ①.

**Off the route**
- A new route that would leave out a stop asks with two clear buttons: **"Über ③"** or **"③ auslassen"** – never
  quietly. The numbered circles look like the points on the map; the turn card shows "② Leiteralm".

**Planning**
- **"PLANEN · Ohne Netz ✓"**: the blue label says whether the route works without a network – ✓ everything on the
  phone, ⟳ loading, ⚠ something missing. A tap explains it.
- **A found place** gets a small card under its pin: **"+ Zur Route"** (on an empty plan **"Hierhin"**) and
  **"⋯ Mehr"** – one tap instead of looking for the menu.

**Words**
- **New words: Plan** (what you plan and save), **Tour** (what you do), **Route** (the calculated way). The list is
  now **"Meine Touren"** with the tabs **"Geplant | Gemacht"**; "Plan speichern", "Plan teilen", "Plan ändern".

**Under the hood**
- Long bike routes measured (113 km): moving one point routes the whole plan again – the faster way (route only the
  changed part) is planned next.
