# OnPath 0.9.0 – the map comes by itself, worldwide overview, junction vibration

**Map and routing data**
- **One loading window** while the map for your area is prepared or downloaded: "Loading the map for your area ·
  about X MB", one percentage that never goes backwards, time left, and Cancel. It opens when you set a point, open
  the app in a new place, search, or plan a route somewhere new.
- **Routing data comes with the map** – in small parts (about 10 MB instead of 200 MB), without a separate step.
- **World overview map** in the app: rivers, lakes, borders, towns and relief everywhere; the detailed map draws on top
  where it is loaded.
- **On a tour, the map ahead loads by itself** – also with the screen off – when you come near the edge of the loaded
  map. It appears after the tour (the map never changes under your feet while navigating); routing data for re-routing
  arrives right away.

**Navigating**
- **Vibration before junctions** of the followed route: 2 short pulses = left, 3 = right, about 40 m before (80 m on a
  bike). Only at real junctions (not at every bend of a path); a chain of hairpins is felt once; nothing when you are off
  the route. Settings → Navigation → "Vibrate at junctions".
- **Lock screen:** the recording notification shows the next junction ("Left in 120 m"), time to go, arrival and
  distance left. Still no button that could end a tour.

**Planning**
- **Point editor:** swipe the plan sheet up – all points in a list: ✕ deletes, drag ≡ to reorder, tap a row to see it on
  the map; elevation profile below.
- **Simple menus:** hold a route point → Start here · Google · Delete; hold beside it → a new point, no question in
  between (a wrong guess: hold again or Undo). Round trips keep "Start & finish here".
- **Vibration** only when you hold the map (1× on the map, 2× on a route point), at the moment you hold.

**Your tours are safe**
- **Backup:** every finished tour and saved route is also written as a GPX file to `Documents/OnPath` (or a folder you
  choose). After a reinstall or on a new phone OnPath offers to restore them. Deleted tours stay 30 days in a recycle
  bin. Settings → Backup; "Export all" as one ZIP.

**Map**
- Ways the router forbids for your activity are never green; private ways neither.
