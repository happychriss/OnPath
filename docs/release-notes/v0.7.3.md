# OnPath 0.7.3 – battery: longer days on one charge

- **Screen off in the pocket.** "Keep screen on while navigating" now uses the proximity sensor: the screen stays
  on while you look at it, goes off when the phone is in your pocket and comes back when you take it out. After a
  minute in the pocket the phone sleeps as usual (recording goes on; press the power button to look again).
- **Pause saves power.** While a tour is paused, GPS rests completely; Resume starts it again (a new piece of the
  track, as before).
- **No Wi-Fi / cell location scans during a tour.** Those positions were never recorded; now they are not asked for
  at all (and the map stops asking once GPS has a fix).
- **Calmer map drawing.** The map draws at most 30 frames per second while navigating (60 while planning) instead of
  up to 120, and the position dot glides at 20 fps.
- **Quieter notification.** The tour clock in the notification runs by itself; the notification is only updated when
  the status or the distance changes.
- Shorter first-point menu: "Start here" · "Go here".
