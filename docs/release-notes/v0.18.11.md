# OnPath 0.18.11 – A stable version: navigation, detours, privacy

**On a tour**
- **Loops whose paths run close together** – the navigation no longer jumps silently to a later part of the loop; when you leave the route it asks as usual.
- **Stops you have passed** are no longer shown as the next stop, and a weak GPS signal no longer counts you as arrived up to 45 m too early.
- **Off-route side** – "turn left/right" is only said when compass and route agree; otherwise the plain sentence, never a wrong side.
- **A failed search for a far-away start** is not started again by the next plan change.

**Planning**
- **Detours stay when you change the plan** – adding or moving a point keeps the detour lines of the other parts; the plan menu has **"Recalculate from scratch"** when the plan holds such lines.
- **My routes keeps its search text** when you come back.

**Sharing your location**
- **The browser view counts down to your next point** and is headed "Shared location".

**Browser planner**
- **Detours stay when you change the plan**, as in the app, with "Recalculate from scratch".
- **Restore your old plan?** – the same question as in the app when a new plan replaces yours.
- **Tapping a POI on a slow phone** works right after moving the map.
- **Opening the pairing link from a phone camera** leads into OnPath; "pair" is the one word for it everywhere.
- Stricter security headers for the page.

**Privacy**
- The privacy texts now say exactly what the app sends: the place search (Photon by komoot), routing, the map region, and what unpairing deletes.

If something does not work as described, please send an export under Settings → Diagnostics.
