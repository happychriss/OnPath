# OnPath 0.18.11 – Everything under your thumb

**A new look for planning and navigating**
- **One card at the bottom** – the search ("Where to?"), the menu ☰, your activity and My tours sit together in one card under your thumb. The top of the map stays free.
- **With a plan** the same card shows time, distance and climb, a 🔍 for the next destination and ☰ – always in the same place. Pull it up for the elevation profile and the points.
- **Messages appear at the top**, right under the status bar, and never cover the Let's go button.
- **Navigating** – Pause and Plan sit in the same floating card; messages and map buttons are green there.
- **Map buttons** in the same order everywhere: compass, position, whole route, eye at the bottom.
- **One calm paper colour** for everything that lies over the map.

**Planning**
- **Menus say what a point becomes**: Set as start · Set as stop · Set as destination – the likeliest one on top, highlighted; Round trip from here always last.
- **Keep a route point** – a named point in the plan can now be kept like a place.
- **Elevation profile**: slide a finger along it to see "1.2 km · 3.4 km left · ↑ 200 m · 8 %"; the area is one green, stronger where it is steep.
- **Stay overnight** – a new filter for places to sleep (huts, bivouacs, camping) beside Food; hotels come through the online search.
- **Hiking levels T1–T5** each show their meaning in one line, so you can read them before you choose.
- **Plans from the browser planner with detour points** are routed through those points.

**On a tour**
- **Paused**: only Continue and End tour. Ending asks Save · Delete (hold for a second) · Cancel.
- **A hold that already vibrated is never lost**, even when the finger slides a little afterwards.
- **Sharing your location** also tells the viewer when you will arrive later than planned.
- The position is the normal blue dot while you change the route; the numbered points stay visible when you stand on them.

If something does not work as described, please send an export under Settings → Diagnostics.
