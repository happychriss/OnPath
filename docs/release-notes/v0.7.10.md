# OnPath 0.7.10 – no upside-down map

- **Fix: the map could point 180° the wrong way** when it turns with the route (field test). When you walk slowly
  against the route ahead – or the position was matched to the wrong part of a path used twice – your own movement
  over the last 15 m now wins, and the map follows the compass / GPS direction instead.
