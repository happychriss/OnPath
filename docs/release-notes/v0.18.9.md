# OnPath 0.18.9 – Symbols answer sooner

**On the map**
- **Tapping or holding a symbol no longer waits for the map to settle** – it answers right after the map has drawn, also while the map follows you.
- **Pressing again no longer sets a stray point** – a second tap or hold is handled after the first has answered, and a real hold is not lost.

Whether the delay you saw is fully gone still needs your check. If a symbol does not answer, please send an export under Settings → Diagnostics.
