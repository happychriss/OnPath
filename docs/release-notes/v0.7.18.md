# OnPath 0.7.18 – compass stays where you put it

- **Fix: after tapping the compass in Navigate, "walking direction up" switched itself back after a few seconds.**
  A leftover check from the old turning method took a camera change by the map for a hand gesture and stopped
  following. Only real gestures (moving or turning the map with your fingers) stop following now.
- If following ever stops without you touching the map, Settings → Diagnostics shows why ("Following stopped: …").
