# OnPath 0.7.2 – route straight ahead, bigger buttons, arrival time, search and sharing

**Walking**
- **The route points straight ahead.** With the map turning with you, it now follows the route ahead of you
  (the next 40 m), so the way is a straight line up the screen. Before, it used the compass when walking slowly,
  which left the map a little turned. Off the route, or when you walk against it, the compass / GPS direction is
  used as before.
- **Bigger map buttons while walking.** Compass, eye and "my position" are separate, bigger round buttons with a
  small gap, so you do not hit the wrong one. Planning keeps the small group.
- **Steadier arrival time.** Progress follows the part of the route you are really walking (also on the way back
  of a round trip and after turning around); the arrival no longer creeps later while you walk. Not on the route
  or no GPS: the card says so ("— not on route", "estimate paused") instead of showing a wrong time.
- **Plan edits during a tour are followed at once.** Round trip, Undo, new points: the edited route is navigated
  as soon as it is computed. An unchanged route keeps your progress. The card names the target.
- Safety: the route check can never cost a recorded point.

**Planning and search**
- **Share a place into OnPath.** In Google Maps (or any app with a place or geo: link) tap Share → OnPath: the map
  flies there and shows the orange ring. A running tour continues.
- **Address search** with street and house number, typo tolerant ("garibalistrasse 13, Merano"). Offline when the
  map package has the address file, otherwise online; online results near the named town come first.
- **Route names** when saving: "Algund → Meran" or "Round trip from Algund" is proposed.

**Map (small)**
- Ground symbols only from zoom 16; own symbols for orchards and vineyards, none on plain farmland.
- Track dots stay dotted in bends; streets and tracks join cleanly at crossings.
