# OnPath 0.7.11 – smooth map, right way up when standing

- **Fix: map 180° the wrong way after standing still.** On a followed route the map turns by the route ahead; after
  about 15 s without progress (standing, very slow) the compass took over, and with the phone tilted or in the
  pocket it pointed backwards. The route direction now stays while you stand on the route.
- **Full frame rate again while you look at the map.** 0.7.3 limited the map to 30 frames per second while
  navigating (and the position dot to 20). Power is saved where nobody looks – screen off, pocket, background – so
  the map runs at the display's full rate again.
