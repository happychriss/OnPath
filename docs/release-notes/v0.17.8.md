# OnPath 0.17.8 – clearer for first-time users

**Setting up the phone**
- **Shorter steps:** the welcome page and the location step say less; the location step names exactly what to pick on
  the next page: "Precise" and "While using the app".
- **One buzz for the vibration test.**
- **Samsung and similar phones:** the battery page says one short sentence and links to a detailed help section on
  onpath.earth; after "Done" or "Later" it no longer comes up before every tour.
- **Settings:** while setup steps are still open, "Phone setup" stands at the top with "N steps open".

**Planning**
- **Hike levels readable on every phone:** the T buttons keep their number, and the chosen level's description shows
  under them.
- **Undo / redo** are two round buttons under the map controls and show only when there is something to undo.
- **Help** is the first entry in the ☰ menu (opens the help on onpath.earth).
