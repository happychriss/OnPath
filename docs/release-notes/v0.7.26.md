# OnPath 0.7.26 – "Take new route" really plans anew

- **"Take new route" now computes a completely new route** from where you are, via the stops you have not reached
  yet, to your destination – like Google Maps. Before, it only found the shortest way back onto the old route, which
  after a small detour looked just like the old route. The old route stays as a thin grey line; walk back onto it near
  where you left it and OnPath follows it again.
- The question says "new route 2.6 km" (and how much longer or shorter it is).
- Following a GPX track (no stops): unchanged – the way back onto the track. If no new route can be found (a stop not
  reachable from here), OnPath offers the way back to the route as before.
