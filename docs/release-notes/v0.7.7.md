# OnPath 0.7.7 – a recording never ends by accident

- **No "Stop & save" in the notification any more.** One tap on the lock screen or in the pocket could end the
  tour. Ending is only in the app, with a question first. Pause / Resume stay in the notification.
- **The tour being recorded cannot be deleted** in My routes (it shows "Recording now").
- **Update during a tour:** Install asks first – installing closes OnPath and recording stops. "After the tour" is
  the suggested choice.
- **Phone restarted or OnPath updated during a tour:** with location "Allow all the time" the recording continues by
  itself; otherwise a notification says so and opens OnPath, which offers Continue. The track so far is always kept.
- Unchanged and confirmed: switching to Plan mode never stops recording.
