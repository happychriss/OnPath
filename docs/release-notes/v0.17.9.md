# OnPath 0.17.9 – a calmer map and the website's colours

**Map**
- **Smoother when moving and zooming:** the map draws with a lighter method, so the short stops while panning and
  zooming are gone.
- **Distance on the locate button** shows whole kilometres from 10 km ("59 km"), so it always fits inside the button.

**Look**
- **Colours like onpath.earth:** the light green of the website behind lists and over the map; a selected choice is
  light green. A light shadow sets the search bar, the map buttons and the bottom sheet off the map.
- **Blue means planning, green means on the way:** in Plan the big go buttons ("Let’s go", "Let’s go on", "Follow this
  tour") are blue; in Navigate everything stays green.
- **Pages fade in and out softly** (My routes, Settings, search, route details). With the phone's animations switched
  off they appear at once as before.

**My routes**
- **Sort:** chips under the search – Newest first (standard), Oldest first, Length, Duration, Ascent. Tap the chosen chip
  again to turn the order round. The app keeps your choice.
- **"Recent" shows one plan:** the one you worked on last. A saved route stays under "Saved" as well; an older unsaved
  plan goes to the recycle bin (restorable for 30 days).

**On the way**
- **"Tour still running":** in Plan during a tour with no route, the sheet says so and offers "Set a new destination".
- **Interrupted tour → Continue** works again when a phone setup step is still open: the setup comes first, then the
  same tour goes on. No second tour can start beside an interrupted one.
- **A route you ended stays ended:** a route calculation that was still running can no longer bring it back.
