# OnPath 0.17.6 – one rule for the tour bar, a status card that never cuts, calm after a phone restart

**Navigating**
- **One rule for the tour bar's buttons:** pale = hold, strong = tap. Weiter follows it too; "Tour beenden" is dark
  green. One hold opens the bar for 10 s; after Weiter it locks again at once.
- **The status card takes the full width when stopped:** "⏸ Angehalten seit 14:32" and "Aufzeichnung und Navigation
  ruhen" now show; at large font nothing is cut, stopped or moving.

**Robust**
- **After a phone restart the app waits for the phone's storage** (up to a minute) instead of saying the maps are
  missing – it never deletes or downloads them again.
- **Backup after a restart:** when the phone's storage is not ready yet, the backup waits and tries again quietly – no
  red "Sicherung fehlgeschlagen" at the start.
- **Updates download even when GitHub's download link fails:** the app fetches the update another way.
