# OnPath 0.6.0

- **Map turns with you, next-turn arrow:** while navigating the map turns with your walking direction (heading up). The round button above the locate button switches to north up and back (the "N" needle means north up). A big green card at the top shows the next turn, e.g. "120 m · Right", and an arrow marks the turn on the map.
- **Search places and addresses:** tap "Search places or addresses" at the top. It finds huts, peaks, villages, stations, trails and addresses offline from the map package; when you are online it also finds street addresses (Photon / OpenStreetMap). Tap a result to see its card: "Add to route" says where it will go before you tap (e.g. "as stop 2 – fits best"), or use As start / As destination. "Google Maps" opens the place in Google Maps for shops, opening times and reviews.
- **Cleaner screen, Google-style:**
  - One search bar at the top with a ⋮ menu for My tours, Import GPX, Settings and Check for updates; the activity ("Hike · T3") is a small chip below it.
  - The plan sheet shows time, distance, ascent / descent, the highest point and the difficulty as plain text, then your stops as named chips (e.g. "Algund › Meraner Hütte › Destination"), undo / redo, and one big button: **Start navigation** (or **Start recording** without a route).
  - The elevation profile and way types / surfaces open with "Elevation & surfaces".
  - Reverse, round trip, start from my location, save, GPX export and clear are in the sheet's ⋯ menu.
  - While a tour records, Plan shows "Tour running · 6.4 km · 1:23:45" with End tour, and the big button says **Continue navigation**.
  - Navigate shows the turn card, then what is left and the recording state at the top; the map buttons sit on the right and "Slide to plan" is at the bottom.
