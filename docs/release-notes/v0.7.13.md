# OnPath 0.7.13 – smoother

- **The map turns smoothly.** Turning with you is now one continuous, gently easing movement at the display's full
  rate, instead of small steps (turn – pause – turn).
- **Your position stays in the same place on screen** when navigating starts, after the position button or after
  looking around – no late 30 % slide any more.
- **Navigate cards glide in and out** (next turn, off-route messages, the re-route question) instead of popping in
  and pushing the other cards.
- **No blue flash when you move the plan map.** The hold ring only appears once you really hold, and fades in.
- **Elevation profile:** the first touch glides the map to the spot instead of jumping; dragging keeps working while
  new GPS fixes arrive.
- Status-bar clock and battery stay dark (readable) on the light map when the phone is in dark mode.
