# OnPath 0.11.6 – bike field fixes

**Navigate**
- **Zooming keeps the direction:** zooming in or out with two fingers no longer switches the map from "direction of
  travel up" to free. Zoom happens around your position; only moving the map or turning it on purpose ends following.
- **Start zoom:** when navigation starts, the map zooms to a fixed level – Hike 17, every bike 16. After that your own
  zoom stays.
- **Time and distance to go side by side**, both large: "1:25 h  18.4 km".
- **Turn round:** when the new route after leaving the route starts by going back the way you came, the question says
  "turn round" and shows a U-turn sign – so a way back on the same road is no longer mistaken for "nothing happened".

**Vibration at junctions**
- New setting (Settings → Navigation): **Off · Always · Screen locked**.
- New pattern: **1 pulse = left, 2 pulses = right** (was 2 / 3), longer pulses that are easier to feel on the bike.
- The event log now says how many junctions the followed route has and whether each cue vibrated – to find why no
  vibration came on the last ride. Note: a followed GPX track has no junctions, only a planned route does.

**Position**
- Under a station roof or indoors: when GPS is weak and the Wi-Fi position is clearly more accurate, the dot uses the
  Wi-Fi position. Recorded tracks stay GPS only.

**Plan**
- "Export GPX" is gone from the route's ⋯ menu – export lives in My routes.
