# OnPath 0.6.9 – tidy map after the tour

- **Saved route leaves the map after the tour.** When a tour ends and its planned route is saved, the route is taken
  off the map. It stays in My routes, and Undo brings it back right away. A route you never saved stays on the map,
  so nothing is lost.
