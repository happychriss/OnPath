# OnPath 0.13.0 – phone setup, step by step

- **Welcome and phone setup.** On the first start OnPath no longer opens Android's location question right away.
  A welcome page offers *Set up now* (about a minute) or *Later*. The guide goes one step at a time and says why each
  thing is needed: your location (precise), notifications, a vibration test, and the battery settings of your phone.
- **Before a tour** OnPath checks what the tour needs. Without precise location it explains how to allow it; things
  that are only recommended can be put off with *Later* (OnPath then asks again after a week at the earliest).
- **Settings → Phone setup** shows everything at a glance – green when set up, amber when recommended, red when a tour
  needs it – with *Set up* for each line.
- **Vibration test:** one buzz = left, two = right. If you feel nothing, OnPath shows where the phone's vibration
  setting is (Pixel: Alarm vibration).
- **Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei / Honor, OnePlus / Oppo / realme:** these phones close apps in the background. OnPath
  shows the settings that keep the recording running.
- **After months without OnPath** Android removes its access. OnPath now says so calmly and asks again.
- **Clearer buttons when you add a place:** "Set as destination" when the route has only a start, "Add as stop 2" or
  "Add as new destination" otherwise – in the search card and when you hold a place on the map.
- OnPath no longer asks for location "all the time". After a phone restart during a tour, open OnPath and tap
  *Continue* – nothing recorded before is lost.
