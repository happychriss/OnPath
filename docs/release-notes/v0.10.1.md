# OnPath 0.10.1 – clearer texts, steadier map loading, fewer surprises

**Map loading**
- **Keeps loading with the screen off** or when you leave the app – a quiet notification shows the progress and goes
  by itself when the map is there.
- **One blue bar at the top** for map downloads. Questions (e.g. "load over mobile data?"), problems ("Try again") and
  "waiting for Wi-Fi" are shown there – the extra map card in the plan sheet is gone.
- **"Maps only on Wi-Fi"** now has a one-time "Load now over mobile data" for when you are out without Wi-Fi.
- **The loading bar follows the time**, not the megabytes – no more racing to 50 % and then standing still.

**Planning**
- **Cancel** while a route is calculated takes back only what started it (a new point, or the activity you changed).
- **Undo and Redo** are both round buttons on the map.
- Search "As start" works like "Start here" on the map.
- "Clear route" also works during a tour (route guidance ends, recording goes on, Undo brings it back).
- Changing the activity in Plan during a tour asks like in Navigate (take the new route or keep it).
- Time details show plain times; the formulas moved to Settings → Diagnostics → Developer tools.

**Tours**
- The bus / car / lift summary also comes when you save on arrival and for an interrupted tour.
- An interrupted tour offers Continue · Delete · Save.
- During a tour: "Continue · Hike" (names the activity), Pause and End tour below.
- After following a saved track, an edited plan is followed again on Continue.

**Texts**
- No technical error texts any more – plain sentences; details only in Settings → Diagnostics.
- One name per thing: Destination, Stop 2, Track (a recording), Touring bike · Trekking bike · E-bike · MTB · E-MTB ·
  Hike, "Direction of travel", End tour.
