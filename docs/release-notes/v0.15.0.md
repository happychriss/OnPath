# OnPath 0.15.0 – Plan in blue, a calmer plan during a tour, the next stop in the turn card

**Planning**
- **Plan is blue**: soft blue side edges and tint tell Plan from Navigate at a glance (Navigate unchanged).
- **Help for the first steps**: a tap on the open map says **"Gedrückt halten, um hier einen Punkt zu setzen."**;
  every map button names itself on a long press.
- **A long route asks**: when the calculation hits its time limit – **"Die Route ist lang, die Berechnung dauert.
  Weiter warten?"** – Weiter warten gives it more time, Abbrechen stops it. No more straight lines instead.
- **Map buttons in one column** in both modes, a little larger (56 dp).

**On the way**
- The **turn card shows the next stop** before the next turn: "1,2 km · Stopp 2 · Leiteralm" or "Ziel · ‹Name›",
  with the plan point's sign.
- The turn card **names the way** like the voice: "Links auf den Radweg".
- **Changing the route during a tour**: the summary row (activity · time · km) above compact bars; one row of three
  **equally high pills – green Weiter · blue Route speichern · red Neu planen** (was "Änderungen verwerfen").
- **Plan is silent**: no voice, no navigation vibration and no messages from movement while you plan – also with the
  screen off; only "Navigation fortsetzen" ends the quiet.
- **Loading a route during a tour asks**: "Route „‹Name›“ laden? Du bist gerade unterwegs." – Mit dieser Route
  weiter · Tour beenden und laden · Abbrechen.

**Words**
- Fewer terms: two states **Planen / Unterwegs**, two things **Route / Tour** – "Aufnahme" and "Navigation" are gone
  from everyday texts.

**Under the hood**
- Holding on the route counts within a finger's width (24 dp, was ~63 dp), so a hold beside the route sets a new point.
- Readable lines on the way (slider line and plan status larger, stronger contrast).
- Settings → Diagnostics: the problem list starts folded – one line, a tap unfolds it.
