# OnPath 0.6.1 – track safety

Nothing new to learn; this update makes recording and navigation harder to break out in the mountains. It comes from a code review of 0.6.0 (robust first).

- **A track that cannot be saved tells you.** If the phone's storage is full or the database is damaged, you get a vibration and a notification "Recording cannot be saved" (and a message in the app) instead of a recording that looks fine but stores nothing. Recording keeps trying; free up space and it continues.
- **A recording that stops says why.** No precise location permission, no GPS, Android refusing the background service: you see "Recording stopped: …" with what to do, instead of "Searching GPS…" for hours. The tour stays, "Continue" is offered next time.
- **Navigate only when recording really runs.** "Start navigation" switches to Navigate once the recording service confirms; if it cannot start, you stay in Plan with a message.
- **Parts of the route not found? You decide.** A plan with dashed straight legs (the router found no way) now asks "Navigate anyway?" before navigating – before, the straight lines were followed silently, including off-route warnings against them.
- **No "You have arrived" at the start of a round trip.** Arrival is asked only after you have been away from the destination, never from a poor fix, and it can ask again if you declined and come back later.
- **No crash when the map style reloads** (theme change, data import) while a tour runs.
- **Better in forest and gorges.** GPS fixes up to 50 m accuracy (was 30 m) are kept, so the track stays whole under trees; standing still with a poor fix adds no distance.
- **Small things:** elapsed time is right after long breaks and on days over 12 h; a damaged database is moved aside instead of deleted; an older test build over a newer database no longer crashes at start; a double tap on Start cannot kill the recording; a map import that fails keeps your old map data.

Also in this build: everything the laptop session merged since 0.6.0 (re-routing questions when you leave the route, time estimates with pace learning, Komoot-style paths and route bands, route menus, activity check in Navigate). See `docs/project-status.md` for their field-test lists.
