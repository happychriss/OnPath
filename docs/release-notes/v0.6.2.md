# OnPath 0.6.2 – POIs where a hike starts, ends and eats

- **Bus stops and food on the map by default.** Bus stops, restaurants, cafés, bars and snack bars now show from zoom 14 without turning anything on in Settings (parking stays off). Zoomed out, the two stops of a pair (both sides of the road) are one symbol; from zoom 16 every stop shows on its own, so you can pick the right side.
- Needs the new map package (South Tyrol, now including the whole Ultental / Zoggler Stausee) for the paired bus stops – older packages show every stop separately at all zooms.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
