# OnPath 0.13.1 – calmer on the handlebar, smarter turns

- **Rest view while cycling.** On the bike the screen shows a calm black page with the next arrow, the distance and
  your arrival time. The map comes back about 20 seconds before each turn, for 30 seconds when you tap, and whenever
  something needs you (off route, arrival, no GPS). Settings → Navigation → *Screen while cycling*: Always on / Smart /
  Like the phone.
- **Vibration only where it matters.** OnPath now vibrates where you could take the wrong way, not where the route
  simply follows the road. Two turns close together get one cue, and the cue comes a few seconds before the turn at
  your speed. On the bike and on foot.
- **Lock screen.** While you follow a route, the next turn, the time left and your arrival show on the lock screen,
  without sound.
- **Starting indoors.** Start a tour inside and OnPath uses your Wi-Fi position until GPS arrives outside, with a note
  "Approximate position". Nothing approximate goes into your track.
- **Phone in a pocket on the bike.** After 5 seconds covered the screen goes off, like on a hike.
- **Phone setup on top** of Settings and in the ☰ menu, in red, when something a tour needs is missing. A small chip
  during a tour tells you when notifications are off.
- **Browser sync runs by itself** while the app is open, and the ☰ menu shows when your phone is paired with a browser.
- **Faster and lighter.** The app needs far less memory with many map areas, from up to 3 GB down to about 0.5 GB,
  and search answers from the first letter.
