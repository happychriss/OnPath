# OnPath 0.11.9 – position on the route, larger arrow

**Navigate**
- **Position on the route** (new, on by default; Settings → Navigation): while you are within about 25 m of the
  route, the dot sits on it – no wobbling beside the road. Further away it shows your real GPS position, so you see
  at once that you left the route. The recording always uses the real GPS position.
- **Larger arrow** at the position dot (where you face).
- **Roundabouts:** the small turn sign on the map no longer says left / right while the card shows the roundabout.
- The diagnostics log now also counts the roundabouts of the followed route.
