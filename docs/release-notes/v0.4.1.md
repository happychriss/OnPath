# OnPath 0.4.1

- **Plan panel can be resized:** swipe it down for a compact view (no elevation profile) or a one-line view with distance, ascent and time; swipe up or tap the handle to enlarge it again.
- **Eye button:** hold it down to hide the route and see the map underneath; release to show the route again.
- **POIs while planning:** tap a POI to add it as a route point, tap again to remove it; long press asks whether to open it in Google Maps.
- **Trail difficulty on the map:** trails show their SAC grade (T1–T6) in small black letters.
- **Map style:** Feldwege (farm and forest tracks) look like Komoot's light lanes with dotted edges; marked hiking routes stand out more from forest and meadows; zoomed out, paths and trails are solid, slightly darker lines; small POI dots are tiny.
