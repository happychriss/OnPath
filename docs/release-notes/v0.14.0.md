# OnPath 0.14.0 – voice guidance, a clearer way to stop, and found places as pins

**Navigation and voice**
- **Voice guidance** (Settings → Navigation → Turn hints: Off · Vibration · Voice, default Voice). On the bike in two
  steps ("In 150 Metern links." – "Gleich links."), on foot "Gleich links." just before the junction. "Los geht's" once
  at the start, "Abseits der Route." / "Wieder auf der Route.", "Neue Route. Bitte umdrehen." when the new way starts
  behind you.
- The **turn card stays on top**: with no turn near it says "↑ Geradeaus · 2,4 km", so nothing jumps.
- **Stops are reached** only by coming near them (25 m on foot, 40 m on the bike); arrival needs every stop and the
  end of the line – no more "Ziel erreicht" too early on round trips.
- Navigate shows the plan's **numbered points**; passed ones turn grey.
- **No GPS while the phone lies still**: nothing is shown – the phone's own motion sensor tells still from moving.
  The warning comes only when you move without GPS.

**Stopping and changing the route on the way**
- Slide **"→ Stopp"** and three tiles appear: **green Pause / Resume, blue Change route, red End**. A tap on the map
  or Back closes them.
- **Changing the route during a tour**: your position shows your activity's sign; at the bottom **green "Navigation
  fortsetzen", blue "Route speichern", red "Änderungen verwerfen"**.
- **Ending**: "Aufnahme beenden?" with "Aufnahme speichern" and, for a new or changed route, a box to save it too.
  Delete asks no second question – it goes to the recycle bin for 30 days.

**Planning**
- The start button says **"Los geht's · ‹destination›"**.
- An empty plan tells you how to begin: **"Wohin soll’s gehen? Halte dein Ziel auf der Karte gedrückt – oder such es
  oben in der Suchleiste."**
- A search result or a place shared from Google Maps is a **blue pin with its name** under it – tap the name for
  "Als Ziel", "Hier starten", "In Google Maps öffnen" or "Nadel entfernen". Shared places are also kept in "Recent".
- Plan buttons as light pills: **"✕ Neu planen" · "⇅ Umkehren" · "↻ Rundtour"**.
- Bike / Gravel on a paved footway: the amber note **"Fußweg – evtl. schieben"** instead of "Schieben".

**Words**
- **Route** = planned, **Tour** = ridden, **Aufnahme** = the running recording. My routes has the tabs **"Geplant" /
  "Unterwegs"**.

**Under the hood**
- Many robustness fixes from the pre-release review: no lost tour on a damaged database, points written later when
  storage is busy, safe resumable downloads (update checked by sha256), no silent sync failures, GPX import cleanups.
- Requests to brouter.de name the app: `OnPath/0.14.0 (+https://onpath.earth)`.
