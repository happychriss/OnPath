# OnPath 0.11.4 – region view, route hints, calmer MTB

**Region view**
- Zoomed far out (a whole region, e.g. Düsseldorf to Bonn) the map now shows main roads, towns, forests, rivers,
  relief and the long-distance bike and hiking routes. The data comes along with your map areas by itself
  (about 3 MB per 200 × 200 km).
- Routes by zoom: far out only long-distance routes, regional ones when you come closer, every signed route close in.

**Hints on the route**
- The plan sheet now tells you what to check before you go: "Ferry 320 m – check times", construction sites, fords,
  and for bikes stiles, kissing gates and turnstiles. Tap the line to see where.
- Long timetabled ferries are only used when you put a point on them; short bike ferries are used automatically.
- MTB and E-MTB: no more "pushing" or "steep" hints – where a sign says no bikes, the line says "no bikes allowed".

**Smaller things**
- Opening a tour from My routes switches to the tour's activity (one grey line tells you).
- Difficulty: a hike with 2 km or more of T2, or a Bike route that is a quarter unpaved or has 5 km of loose ground, is
  at least "Moderate".
