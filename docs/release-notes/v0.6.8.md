# OnPath 0.6.8 – trail safety

**Routes avoid what you should not walk or ride**
- **Hiking:** trails above your T level are never used any more; before, they were only expensive. Via ferratas,
  abandoned paths and paths marked "not visible" are never used either. Faint paths, informal paths and fords count
  as longer. Marked trails up to T3 are now preferred over unmarked paths.
- **Bike (touring, trekking, e-bike):** no via ferratas and no mountain paths of T3 or harder. T2 paths only when
  there is no other way.
- **MTB:** no via ferratas and nothing of T4 or harder.
- **Route stops short of a point:** if your point can only be reached by a way you may not use (e.g. a summit above
  your T level), the plan now says so: "The route ends 380 m away from point 3: the last part is too difficult …".

**Navigate**
- **Repeated reminder:** while the "Off route – new route?" question is open, the phone vibrates again about every
  150 m or 30 s.
- **"Hairpin left / right"** instead of "U-turn".
- **"Straight line":** the arrow back to the route says so. It is a direction only, not a path.
- **Your position for an emergency call:** open the status card (chevron) to see your coordinates, accuracy and
  altitude. Tap the line to copy the coordinates.
- **No position jumps:** after the first GPS fix, the rough phone network position (often far off in the mountains)
  is no longer used. Instead you see "Waiting for GPS · last fix 4 min ago".
