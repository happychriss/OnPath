# OnPath 0.7.0 – the new map

One map style instead of "normal" and "high contrast", built from a colour analysis after the field test
(docs/design/map-colour-analysis.md). **Import the new map part too** (see below), otherwise paths look as before.

- **Marked routes:** a transparent green band (#168403 at 40 %) without edges. It stays visible on forest,
  meadow and rock, and the path on top stays easy to read. Route pieces are merged, so the band no longer gets
  darker where they meet.
- **No more white lines:**
  - Paths are thin black lines, mountain trails black dots and alpine trails dot-dash.
  - Small streets are two thin grey lines.
- **Forest and farm roads:** brown.
  - Zoomed out: one dashed line.
  - From zoom 15: two dotted lines. The spacing shows the surface: close = compact, wide = soft or grass.
  - Paved farm roads look like streets.
- **Ground:**
  - Forest and meadow are lighter.
  - Settlements are light grey.
  - The relief shadow is greenish instead of blue-grey.
  - Faint symbols from zoom 14 for wet meadow, farmland, rock and sand.
- **Cable cars:** ropes are wider when zoomed in.

**Map data:** download the new map part (42 MB) from the test downloads page and import it in Settings → Map data.
The terrain part stays the same. With the old map part the app still works and draws paths as before.
