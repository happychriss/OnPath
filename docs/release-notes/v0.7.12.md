# OnPath 0.7.12 – map turns right away after Plan

- **Fix: after Plan → Navigate the map could stay north up** (looked "180° turned" when walking south). It waited
  for the walking direction to change by a few degrees – walking straight it never did. Now the current direction
  is passed on at once and with every GPS fix, and the north-up turn from Plan no longer runs into Navigate.
- Reminder: since 0.7.11 the map runs at the display's full frame rate again (up to 120 Hz) while you look at it.
