# OnPath 0.10.0 – clearer map, smoother planning, bus and lift rides, simpler Settings

**After installing:** your downloaded map areas update once by themselves – new map colours and sharper relief. This
needs about 2 MB more per area.

**Map**
- **The map shows what the route planner does:** green = good for your activity, black = possible, a **grey line = too
  difficult** for it (also via ferratas – they keep their ticks – and steps for mountain bikes), **red only where access
  is not allowed**.
- **Hiking routes above your T level** are drawn as grey dashes instead of green.
- **Balanced hill shading** and sharper relief when you zoom in.
- The map shows a short "© OpenStreetMap"; tap it for the full credits.

**Planning**
- **Smoother plan sheet:** three steps (small, half, full), moves smoothly with your finger.
- **Activity picker with the hiking level (T1–T5)**; the app remembers your last activity and level.
- **Five fitness levels** for the time estimate: Relaxed · Easy · Average · Fit · Very fit. E-bikes stay at about
  25 km/h on the flat.
- **Points the route cannot reach** stay where you put them, joined to the route by a grey dashed line; one red line in
  the plan sheet tells you, with Show.
- **Route warnings:** one amber line in the plan sheet lists what is worth knowing on the route (steep parts, pushing
  the bike, steps, trails above your level), with a list of the spots. After you change the activity, a note says the
  route changed.
- **Search:** one list with the best results first, addresses show the village; online results that arrive late are
  added below, so nothing moves under your finger. A place shared from another app shows a small note when its position
  comes from the address.

**On a tour**
- **Plan ⇄ Navigate** now switch with a short, smooth animation.
- **Route back to the start?** – if you delete the goal of a two-point plan during a tour, OnPath asks whether to guide
  you back to the start.
- **Deleting all points** ends the route guidance; the recording goes on, Undo brings everything back. Navigate shows
  only the route line, no numbered points.
- **Bus, car or lift during a tour:** nothing changes during the ride; at End tour you choose how to save it (only up to
  the ride, all of it without counting the rides, split into two tours, or as recorded). Your recorded points are always kept.
- **Time to arrival** learns your pace during each tour only and settles calmly after plan changes.
- **Saves battery:** slower GPS while you stand still – no point is lost.

**Downloads**
- **Map downloads follow your plan:** areas for a route you no longer have stop loading. A blue strip shows what loads in
  the background, with "Cancel all".

**Settings**
- **Settings is now a list of topics**, each with its own page.
- **New Privacy page.** Online search sends only a rough position (about 1 km), never your exact location.
- **Offline maps → Clean up** shows what it would remove (and what stays: your plan, saved routes, a running tour, where
  you are) before anything is removed. **Clear all** removes every downloaded area after a second confirmation.

**Faster and more robust:** quicker routing on long routes, fewer map reloads, a smoother elevation profile, better
diagnostics.
