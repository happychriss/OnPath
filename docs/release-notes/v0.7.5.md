# OnPath 0.7.5 – pause keeps an eye on you

- **GPS stays on during a pause, just slower** (every 10 s instead of every second; 0.7.3 switched it off).
- **Forgot to resume?** If you walk more than 150 m away while the tour is paused, the phone vibrates and a
  notification asks "Recording is paused – You are moving – resume recording?" with a Resume button. Recording
  never resumes by itself.
