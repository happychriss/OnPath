# OnPath 0.11.7 – position dot, U-turn sign, roundabouts

**Navigate**
- **The dot is where you are:** after the screen was off or locked, the position dot no longer glides from the old
  place – it shows your position at once.
- **Larger position dot**, and the turn badge on the map is lighter and a little smaller, so the two are easy to tell
  apart.
- **U-turn sign on the map:** when the new route starts by turning round, a U-turn sign marks the place – while asking
  and after taking the route, until you have turned and ridden on.
- **Roundabouts with the exit:** the turn card shows a roundabout sign and "Roundabout · exit 2"; the lock screen says
  it too.
