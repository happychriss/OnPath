# OnPath 0.5.0

- **Two modes: Plan and Navigate.** The app opens in Plan: build or change your route, look at tours and settings. "Start navigation" switches to Navigate – the map follows you and taps on the map change nothing. Without a route, navigation simply records your track.
- **Slide to plan:** the only way out of Navigate is the "Slide to plan" switch at the bottom – drag it almost all the way. Pocket or glove touches change nothing. Your recording keeps running in Plan.
- **Continue navigation re-routes:** back in Plan during a tour you can change the route; "Continue navigation" plans again from where you are and skips the stops you have already passed.
- **Arrival:** near the destination the app asks "You have arrived – end tour?" (End & save / Keep going). In Plan you can end a tour any time with "End tour".
- **Cleaner screen:** My tours and Settings are in the top bar, the GPS status is a small dot on the locate button, the eye button and the Plan / Record / My tours bar are gone. Off-route warnings only come while navigating.
