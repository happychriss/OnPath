# OnPath 0.13.3 – Save like Word, better tour edits

- **Places shared from Google Maps** land at the right place, never at a namesake in another town. If it's not
  clear, a box says "Place not clear" and offers the search.
- **Search:** the list no longer jumps while you type. Six short filters: Places · Food · Sights · Water · Transit ·
  Parking.
- **Browser pairing:** the phone says honestly when the browser unpaired ("Unpaired in the browser") and pairs again
  with one scan.
- **Guided to a far route ("Guide me"):** adding a point on the way works, Undo brings the guidance back, and there
  are no turn cues while you're still far from the route.
- **"Show whole route"** button in Plan and Navigate.
- **Changing the plan during a tour:** your position splits the plan into the part you've passed and the part ahead.
  Delete a point ahead and the tour goes on from where you are.
- **Save like in Word:** Save saves at once. Tap the name to rename it; "Save as new route" is in ⋯.
- **Short trips** no longer take big detours (one case: 2.1 km → 1.1 km).
- **During a tour the plan sheet is one compact row**, so the map stays free.
- **The locate button in Navigate** goes back to the normal navigation zoom.
- **Small fixes:** the whole route shows after opening a plan link; destination names wrap onto two lines; no more
  "Steps · 0 m"; "New route · was …" comes after Undo.
- **Safer saving:** all files are replaced in one step, so a crash can't leave half a file behind.
- **The browser computes routes exactly like the app**, also in tricky spots.
