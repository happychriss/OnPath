# OnPath 0.12.0 – plan from a track, navigation on the lock screen

**Plan**
- **A GPX track becomes a plan:** open a track (imported, or one you rode) and tap **Plan a route from it**. OnPath
  re-traces it with its own router and sets as few route points as needed – the ride Rosellen → Düsseldorf became 7
  points that follow the track to 99 %. Then edit it like any plan; Undo brings the old plan back.
- A planned route's GPX now carries its route points (start, stops, goal) as waypoints.

**Navigate**
- **On the lock screen:** while a tour runs, a short press of the power button shows the navigation – no unlocking.
  To get to the plan (Edit route) the phone asks to unlock.
- **Settings on the way:** the ⚙ button in Navigate opens the navigation settings (vibration, position on the route,
  screen on …).
- **Fewer vibrations on the bike:** slight bends no longer vibrate; turns and forks still do.
- **Larger arrow** at the position dot with north up too.

**Recording**
- **Heights above sea level:** recorded heights were about 45 m too high (GPS heights are above the ellipsoid, not the
  sea). Now the phone's sea-level height is used; the first seconds after the start, while the GPS height settles,
  get no height.
