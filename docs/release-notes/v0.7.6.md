# OnPath 0.7.6 – look around while navigating

- **Turn the map by hand while navigating.** Turn it with two fingers (or move it) to match the mountains in front of
  you. The map holds still while you look. As soon as you walk on (about 25 m), it comes back to your position and
  orientation by itself. The compass or the position button bring it back at once, as before.
