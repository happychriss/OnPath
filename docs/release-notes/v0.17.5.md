# OnPath 0.17.5 – "Anhalten" instead of "Pause", a clearer stopped tour

**Navigating**
- **"Pause" is now "Anhalten":** the tour's bar reads ⏸ Anhalten | ✎ Planen; while stopped the app says
  "Angehalten" – on the screen and in the notification.
- **The stopped tour in the status card:** no band at the bottom any more – the card's head says
  "⏸ Angehalten seit 14:32" (and "Aufzeichnung und Navigation ruhen" where it fits), a thin line, then the tour so far
  ("Bisher 5,3 km · in Bewegung 1:12").
- **Three clear buttons with icons:** ▶ Weiter · ■ Tour beenden · ✎ Planen (in German Weiter over the other two, at
  large font one under the other). Weiter still wants a hold on the locked bar.
