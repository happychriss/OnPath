# OnPath 0.8.2 – route bands visible on streets

- **Hiking and bike routes on small streets are visible again:** the green route band is now drawn over streets and
  roads (the street shows green-tinted, like Komoot), still under paths and trails. Before, the street covered the
  band and only two thin pale edges were left – routes like the Sissiweg or AVS 10 / 10A in Meran-Obermais looked missing.
- No new map download needed – the change is in the app's map style.
