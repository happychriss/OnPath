# OnPath 0.11.2 – quieter town map, clearer bike names, better bike paths

**Map**
- Towns are much quieter: zoomed out, the many small path pieces in towns are gone and you see the street grid on grey
  built-up land. Close in, footways and sidewalks in towns are thin and grey; paths in parks, forests and the
  mountains stay dark and clear for hiking.
- Paths no longer stop just short of the street – the small missing piece at path ends is drawn.
- Ways you may not use (private yards, driveways, closed tracks) are marked more calmly: a thin red dash instead of a
  wide red band, never louder than a normal street. Closed paths and tracks stay clearly marked.
- For bikes, signed cycle routes are thin lines (teal; violet for long-distance routes), and bike-friendly ways have a
  soft green glow when you zoom in.
- **Your map areas are rebuilt once** to get the new look; this happens by itself when you use them.

**Bike routes**
- Shared foot / cycle paths with a firm gravel surface ("compacted") now count as good bike ways – routes no longer
  zig-zag through side streets to avoid them.
- **Gravel bike** (was Trekking) now keeps off busy roads like **Bike** does. The only difference between the two:
  Bike avoids gravel, Gravel bike is happy on it.

**Clearer names**
- The bike activities are now **Bike** (was Touring bike) and **Gravel bike** (was Trekking bike), and every activity
  in the picker has one line saying what it is for. Your saved routes keep their activity.
