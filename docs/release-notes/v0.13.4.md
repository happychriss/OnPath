# OnPath 0.13.4 – Shared places, privacy, pairing on tour

- **Places shared from Google Maps:** OnPath no longer opens the Google page. It uses the link itself, its own search
  and the phone's address service, and the place must lie in the town that was shared.
- **Privacy:** a clearer privacy page, and a new "Licenses" page in Settings → About.
- **Pairing with the browser during a tour** works now. The phone says honestly what is going on: "Waiting for the
  browser …" and "Routes sync after the tour".
- **Found or shared places** get a new marker: a dashed blue ring with a preview line to the route.
- **Adding a destination from the place card** shows the whole new route. "Show whole route" also fits better when
  the sheet is pulled up.
- **The scale bar** shows the right length now.
- **English texts** use US spelling (center, kilometer, canceled).
- **Offline:** map areas beside the route load quietly in the background, without a false "no detailed map", and
  starting a tour secures the map along the route again.
