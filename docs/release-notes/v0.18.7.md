# OnPath 0.18.7 – Symbols, rides and a clearer voice

**On the map**
- **Tapping or holding a symbol finds it more reliably** – when the app's list of symbols is out of date, it now asks the map before it treats the press as an empty spot.

**After a tour**
- **A ride in your tour? OnPath asks when you save** – with the ride or without it. Splitting into two tours is no longer offered at this point.
- **Editing the rides updates the tour everywhere** – the picture in the list, the overview and the line on the map.

**Navigation**
- **Off the route, the voice says the side and the distance when it is sure** („Die Route liegt rechts, 40 Meter.“). When it is not sure, you hear the plain sentence.
- **A start far away** – from 25 km a line warns you, and Abbrechen during the search goes back to the question.

**Planning**
- **Clearer day rows on multi-day tours** – the day in bold, the values on the right, from and to when both places are known.
- **Holding the eye on a round-trip suggestion** now hides the suggested loop too, so you see the way under it.
