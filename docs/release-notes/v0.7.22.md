# OnPath 0.7.22 – compass cleaned up

Found by a review of all changes of the last two days:
- **No more jumping back after search or showing a route.** The 0.7.20 safety net brought the map back to your
  position after OnPath itself moved the map (search result, opening a route). It is gone – the real cause was fixed
  in 0.7.21.
- **Compass after looking around (Navigate):** if you turned or moved the map by hand, the compass now brings back
  the direction you chose (walking direction up), instead of switching to north.
- **Walking direction → north up (Navigate):** the map turns north once, smoothly, and keeps following you.
- Settings: "Map direction (Plan and Navigate)" – one setting for both modes.
