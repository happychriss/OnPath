# OnPath 0.7.1 – compass, profile dot, back to your position

- **One compass for planning and navigating.** If you turned the map by hand, tap the compass for north up. Tap
  again and the map turns with your walking direction and follows you. Tap once more for north up. Without a GPS
  fix the app says so instead of turning. While you plan, adding a point stops the turning, so the map holds still.
- **Back to your position.** If you open the phone again during a tour (e.g. from your pocket), the map jumps back
  to where you are.
- **Elevation profile on the map (Navigate).** Open the status card and move your finger along the profile. A dot
  shows that spot on the route and the map moves there. Lift the finger: the dot stays 4 seconds, then the map
  returns to you.
- **Position dot always on top.** The search ring no longer covers your position.
- **Clear route after the tour.** If a route you never saved is still on the map, the "Tour saved" window offers
  "Clear route from map". Undo brings it back.
