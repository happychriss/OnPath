# OnPath 0.18.10 – Share your location, detours explained

**On a tour**
- **Share location for everyone** – the new tour bubble above the map bubble has a share button: others follow your position in the browser while you are on the way. Tap it again to stop sharing. The ⚙ next to it opens the tour settings.
- **GPS rests while the phone lies still** – the motion sensor decides: GPS jumps indoors no longer wake the recording, and after an hour without moving the GPS switches off until you move again.
- **A single GPS jump no longer misleads the navigation** – a jump onto the route ahead no longer causes a false off-route or arrival message, and no longer changes the arrival time.
- **Clock set back during a tour** – "to go" and the arrival time start anew instead of standing still for an hour.
- **A tour continued after the app was closed by the system** counts the full distance of its saved points.

**Planning**
- **Detours explained in plain words** – "17.4 km detour needed, ② only reachable from Hiking T2", and for a stop the level reaches over a detour: "② adds a 2.8 km detour – shorter with Hiking T2".
- **Restore the old plan** – when a new plan replaces the one you had, OnPath asks whether you want the old one back.
- **Recent keeps the activity and level** of a plan that was replaced.
- **Plans with detour points from the browser planner** open and are routed through those points. The app does not set such points itself yet.

**Browser planner**
- The wait window now blocks the map while it is open, as in the app.

If something does not work as described, please send an export under Settings → Diagnostics.
