# OnPath 0.17.0 – a calmer plan sheet, a locked Navigate, long bike routes faster

**Planning**
- **A calmer plan sheet.** The activity is a button on the map – **one tap** chooses it, Hike's **T1–T5** sit right
  under its row, no confirm. The sheet keeps one light blue row (**✕ Neu planen · ⇅ Umkehren · ↻ Rundtour**), the
  route's numbers and profile, and **one green button "Los geht's"**. Saving is in **☰** (also during a tour).
- **✕ asks before anything is lost:** **"Plan speichern?"** – Verwerfen · Abbrechen · Speichern.
- **During a tour** the plan has a light blue band with a round **←** back to the tour; there is no green button.

**Long bike routes faster**
- The route is calculated **leg by leg**, and an edit routes **only the legs it changed** – moving one point of a
  98 km plan no longer routes the whole plan again. The same points give the same route as before.
- During a tour, an edit no longer routes every point ahead twice: the way to the next point reuses the plan's legs.
- The wait box shows how far it is: **"Abschnitt 2 von 4 wird berechnet…"**, and the bar moves within one long leg too.

**Navigate**
- **Locked against pocket taps:** the slider only unlocks (**» Entsperren**); then **Pause** and **Tour beenden** are in
  the status card and one big blue **Plan** button changes the route. An open lock beside it locks again. Settings →
  Navigation can switch the lock off.
- The **next 2–3 stops** are under the profile, with their arrival time, and as small numbered signs on the profile.

**Ending and clearing**
- **Tour beenden** is one sheet: the tour (numbers, a big green **Tour speichern**), what happens to the plan, and
  **Weiterwandern / Weiterfahren**; **Tour löschen** is quiet at the bottom and goes to the bin.
- **Clearing the plan during a tour** says first **"Plan leeren? Deine Tour läuft weiter …"**: the guidance ends, the
  recording never stops – Navigate shows **"Freie Tour · nur Aufnahme"**.

**Browser planner**
- plan.onpath.earth calculates leg by leg with the same code and shows the same "Abschnitt n von m" line, so a plan
  from the browser and from the phone routes the same.
